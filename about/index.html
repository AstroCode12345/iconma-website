About

Islamic Center of Newton, founded in 2024.

Our mission

The mission of the Islamic Center of Newton is to:

  • Provide a welcoming space for Muslims in the city of Newton, MA and surrounding cities and towns to practice their faith, and engage in religious, social, charitable, and educational activities.

  • Promote the understanding and application of Islamic teachings through worship, learning, and community service.

  • Foster unity, inclusion, and mutual respect within the local and broader community.

  • Engage in outreach to enhance the understanding of Islam and foster peaceful interfaith relationships.
